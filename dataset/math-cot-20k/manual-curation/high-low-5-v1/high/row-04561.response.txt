The sums involving binomial coefficients multiplied by powers of k can be evaluated by differentiating the binomial theorem:

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{k} x^k = (1+x)^n.
$$

Differentiating once brings down a factor of k:

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{k} k x^{k-1} = n(1+x)^{n-1}.
$$

Multiplying by x and evaluating at x = 1 gives

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{k} k x^k = nx(1+x)^{n-1},
\qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{k} k = n2^{n-1}.
$$

The k = 0 term contributes zero in each of the three weighted sums, so their lower limits can be written as 1.

For the quadratic term, differentiate nx(1+x)^{n-1} using the product rule:

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{k} k^2 x^{k-1}
= n(1+x)^{n-1}+n(n-1)x(1+x)^{n-2}.
$$

Multiplying by x gives

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{k} k^2 x^k
= nx(1+x)^{n-1}+n(n-1)x^2(1+x)^{n-2}.
$$

At x = 1, this becomes

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{k} k^2
= n2^{n-1}+n(n-1)2^{n-2}
= 2^{n-2}[2n+n(n-1)]
= n(n+1)2^{n-2}.
$$

For the cubic term, differentiate the preceding expression term by term. The derivative of the first term is

$$
n(1+x)^{n-1}+n(n-1)x(1+x)^{n-2}.
$$

The derivative of the second term is

$$
n(n-1)[2x(1+x)^{n-2}+(n-2)x^2(1+x)^{n-3}].
$$

Combining the terms proportional to x(1+x)^{n-2}, and multiplying the derivative by x, yields

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{k} k^3 x^k
= nx(1+x)^{n-1}
+3n(n-1)x^2(1+x)^{n-2}
+n(n-1)(n-2)x^3(1+x)^{n-3}.
$$

Factor out n(1+x)^{n-3}. The remaining polynomial is

$$
x(1+2x+x^2)+3(n-1)x^2(1+x)+(n-1)(n-2)x^3.
$$

The coefficient of x² is 2 + 3(n-1) = 3n-1, and the coefficient of x³ is

1 + 3(n-1) + (n-1)(n-2) = 1 + 3n - 3 + n² - 3n + 2 = n².

Thus

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{k} k^3 x^k
= n(1+x)^{n-3}[x+(3n-1)x^2+n^2x^3].
$$

Evaluating at x = 1 gives

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} \mathrm{C}_{n}^{k} k^3
= n2^{n-3}[1+(3n-1)+n^2]
= n2^{n-3}(n^2+3n)
= n^2(n+3)2^{n-3}.
$$

Substitute the three identities into the requested expression:

$$
2[n^2(n+3)2^{n-3}]
-3n[n(n+1)2^{n-2}]
+n^2[n2^{n-1}].
$$

Factoring out 2^{n-2} gives

$$
2^{n-2}[n^2(n+3)-3n^2(n+1)+2n^3].
$$

The bracket simplifies to

$$
n^3+3n^2-3n^3-3n^2+2n^3=0.
$$

So the expression is zero. As one numerical check, for n = 2 the sums weighted by k, k², and k³ are respectively 4, 6, and 10. The expression is then 2*10 - 3*2*6 + 2²*4 = 20 - 36 + 16 = 0.
