### Key Concept: Median Minimizes Sum of Absolute Deviations

For a function of the form:

$$
f(x) = \sum_{i=1}^n |x - a_i|
$$

the value of $ x $ that minimizes $ f(x) $ is the **median** of the set $ \{a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n\} $. This is because the derivative of the sum (where it exists) is the number of points less than $ x $ minus the number of points greater than $ x $, and the minimum occurs when these two numbers are equal—i.e., at the median.
