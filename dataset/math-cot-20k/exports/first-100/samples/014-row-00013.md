### **Step 1: Understand the Structure**

We are given 8 **distinct** elements. These need to be arranged in **two rows of 4 elements each**, with the following constraints:

- **Element A and Element B** must be in the **front row**.
- **Element C** must be in the **back row**.

The remaining 5 elements (let’s call them D, E, F, G, H) can be in either row, provided the total in each row is 4 and the constraints are satisfied.

---

### **Step 2: Determine the Composition of the Rows**

- The **front row** must contain **A**, **B**, and **2 more elements** from the remaining 5.
- The **back row** must contain **C**, and **3 more elements** from the remaining 5.

So, from the 5 remaining elements (D, E, F, G, H), we must choose 2 to go to the **front row**, and the other 3 will go to the **back row**.

\binom{5}{2} = 10

---

### **Step 4: Compute the Total Number of Arrangements**

$$
\text{Total} = \binom{5}{2} \times 4! \times 4! = 10 \times 24 \times 24 = 5760
