- Back to $ F(f, 0) $

Using the shoelace formula:

$$
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \left| \sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i) \right|
$$

Compute each term:

- $ f \cdot \frac{h}{2} - a \cdot 0 = \frac{f h}{2} $
- $ a \cdot h - a \cdot \frac{h}{2} = \frac{a h}{2} $
- $ a \cdot h - 0 \cdot h = a h $
- $ 0 \cdot 0 - f \cdot h = -f h $

Total sum:

$$
\frac{f h}{2} + \frac{a h}{2} + a h - f h = \frac{f h}{2} + \frac{a h}{2} + a h - f h = \frac{3 a h - f h}{2}
$$

So the area is:

$$
\text{Area}_{FECD} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{3 a h - f h}{2} = \frac{3 a h - f h}{4}
$$

Substitute the known values $ a h = 86 $ and $ f h = 30 $:

$$
\text{Area}_{FECD} = \frac{3 \cdot 86 - 30}{4} = \frac{258 - 30}{4} = \frac{228}{4} = 57
$$
