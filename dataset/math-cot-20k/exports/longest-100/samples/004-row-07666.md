### Step 1: Understanding the Condition

The condition can be rephrased as follows:
- For any two elements $ m < n $ in $ X $, the ratio $ \frac{n}{m} $ must be a perfect square, and the square root of that ratio must also be an element of $ X $.

This means the structure of the set $ X $ must be such that the ratio between any two elements is the square of another element in the set.
