$$
\mathbb{E}\left[\sum_{i=1}^{12} X_i\right] = \sum_{i=1}^{12} \mathbb{E}[X_i]

There are 12 edges in total, each with an independent probability of $ \frac{4}{9} $ of being monochromatic. Therefore, the expected number of same-color edges is:
$$
\mathbb{E}[\text{same-color edges}] = 12 \cdot \frac{4}{9} = \frac{48}{9} = \frac{16}{3}
$$
