For a binomial variable $Y \sim \text{Bin}(n, p)$, we have:

$$
\mathbb{E}[Y] = np, \quad \text{Var}(Y) = np(1 - p), \quad \mathbb{E}[Y^2] = \text{Var}(Y) + (\mathbb{E}[Y])^2 = np(1 - p) + (np)^2.
