To determine the equation of the **circumscribed circle** (or **circumcircle**) of a triangle defined by the lines:

$$
y = x + 1, \quad y = -\frac{1}{2}x - 2, \quad y = 3x - 9
$$

we proceed as follows:

---

### Step 1: Find the Vertices of the Triangle

The triangle is formed by the intersections of each pair of the three given lines.

#### Intersection of $ y = x + 1 $ and $ y = -\frac{1}{2}x - 2 $:

$$
x + 1 = -\frac{1}{2}x - 2 \Rightarrow \frac{3}{2}x = -3 \Rightarrow x = -2
$$
$$
y = -2 + 1 = -1 \Rightarrow \text{Point A: } (-2, -1)
$$

#### Intersection of $ y = -\frac{1}{2}x - 2 $ and $ y = 3x - 9 $:

$$
-\frac{1}{2}x - 2 = 3x - 9 \Rightarrow -\frac{7}{2}x = -7 \Rightarrow x = 2
$$
$$
y = 3(2) - 9 = -3 \Rightarrow \text{Point B: } (2, -3)
$$

#### Intersection of $ y = x + 1 $ and $ y = 3x - 9 $:

$$
x + 1 = 3x - 9 \Rightarrow -2x = -10 \Rightarrow x = 5
$$
$$
y = 5 + 1 = 6 \Rightarrow \text{Point C: } (5, 6)
$$

So the triangle has vertices at:
- $ A = (-2, -1) $
- $ B = (2, -3) $
- $ C = (5, 6) $

---

### Step 2: Find the Circumcenter

The **circumcenter** is the center of the circle that passes through all three points. It is the intersection of the **perpendicular bisectors** of two sides of the triangle.

#### Perpendicular Bisector of AB

- Midpoint of AB: 
  $$
  \left( \frac{-2 + 2}{2}, \frac{-1 + (-3)}{2} \right) = (0, -2)
  $$
- Slope of AB: 
  $$
  \frac{-3 - (-1)}{2 - (-2)} = \frac{-2}{4} = -\frac{1}{2}
  $$
- Slope of perpendicular bisector: $ 2 $

Using point-slope form:
$$
y - (-2) = 2(x - 0) \Rightarrow y = 2x - 2 \quad \text{(Equation 1)}
$$

#### Perpendicular Bisector of AC

- Midpoint of AC: 
  $$
  \left( \frac{-2 + 5}{2}, \frac{-1 + 6}{2} \right) = \left( \frac{3}{2}, \frac{5}{2} \right)
  $$
- Slope of AC: 
  $$
  \frac{6 - (-1)}{5 - (-2)} = \frac{7}{7} = 1
  $$
- Slope of perpendicular bisector: $ -1 $

Using point-slope form:
$$
y - \frac{5}{2} = -1\left(x - \frac{3}{2}\right) \Rightarrow y = -x + 4 \quad \text{(Equation 2)}
$$

#### Solve for the Center

Set the two equations equal to find the intersection (center):

$$
2x - 2 = -x + 4 \Rightarrow 3x = 6 \Rightarrow x = 2
$$
$$
y = 2(2) - 2 = 2
$$

So the **center** of the circle is at $ (2, 2) $.

---

### Step 3: Find the Radius

Use the distance formula from the center to any vertex (e.g., point A):

$$
r^2 = (2 - (-2))^2 + (2 - (-1))^2 = 4^2 + 3^2 = 16 + 9 = 25 \Rightarrow r = 5
$$

---

### Step 4: Write the Equation of the Circle

Using the standard form of a circle:

$$
(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2
$$

Substitute $ h = 2 $, $ k = 2 $, $ r^2 = 25 $:

$$
(x - 2)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 25
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{(x - 2)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = 25}
$$
