To solve the trigonometric equation:

$$
\sin x \cdot \cos 2x \cdot \cos 4x = 1,
$$

we begin by analyzing the possible values of the left-hand side. Each of the three functions—$\sin x$, $\cos 2x$, and $\cos 4x$—has a range of $[-1, 1]$. Therefore, the product of these three terms has a maximum absolute value of 1. For the product to equal **1**, it must be that each of the three functions is either $1$ or $-1$, and the number of negative terms must be even (so that the product is positive).

$$
\sin x = a,\quad \cos 2x = b,\quad \cos 4x = c,\quad \text{with } a, b, c \in \{-1, 1\},\quad \text{and } a \cdot b \cdot c = 1.
$$
