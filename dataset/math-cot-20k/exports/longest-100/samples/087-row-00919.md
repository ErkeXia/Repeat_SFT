Between the first two points:
$$
\text{Distance} = \sqrt{(1.5 - (1 - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}))^2 + (1 - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - 0.5)^2}
= \sqrt{(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2})^2 + (\frac{1}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2})^2}
$$

Each of these squared terms simplifies to $\frac{1}{4} + \frac{3}{4} = 1$, so the total is:
$$
\sqrt{1 + 1} = \sqrt{2}
$$

Thus, **each side** of the shaded square is $\sqrt{2}$, and all four sides are equal.

The **diagonals** are:
$$
\sqrt{[(1 + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}) - (1 - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2})]^2 + [1.5 - 0.5]^2} = \sqrt{(\sqrt{3})^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{3 + 1} = 2
$$

Since the diagonals are equal and the sides are equal and at right angles, the shape is a **square**.

### Step 4: Calculating the Area

The side length of the square is $\sqrt{2}$, so the area is:
$$
\text{Area} = (\sqrt{2})^2 = 2
$$
