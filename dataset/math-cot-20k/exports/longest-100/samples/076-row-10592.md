- Slope of $ BE $ is:
  $$
  \frac{\frac{2cb}{2c + m}}{\frac{2c(a + m)}{2c + m}} = \frac{b}{a + m}
  $$
- Slope of $ AD $ is:
  $$
  \frac{0 - b}{c - a} = \frac{-b}{c - a}
  $$

Setting the product of slopes to $ -1 $:

$$
\frac{b}{a + m} \cdot \frac{-b}{c - a} = -1
\Rightarrow \frac{b^2}{(a + m)(c - a)} = 1
\Rightarrow b^2 = (a + m)(c - a)
$$

---

### Step 3: Coordinates of Point $ F $

Point $ F $ is the intersection of $ BE $ and $ AD $. Using parametric equations and solving the system, the coordinates of $ F $ are:

$$
F = \left( \frac{c(a + m)}{m + c}, \frac{cb}{m + c} \right)
$$

---

### Step 4: Area of Triangle $ DEF $

Using the **shoelace formula** for area with points:

- $ D = (c, 0) $
- $ E = \left( \frac{2c(a + m)}{2c + m}, \frac{2cb}{2c + m} \right) $
- $ F = \left( \frac{c(a + m)}{m + c}, \frac{cb}{m + c} \right) $

After simplifying the area expression, we find:

$$
S_{DEF} = \frac{c^2 b m}{2(m + 2c)(m + c)}
$$

We are given that this equals 5:

$$
\frac{c^2 b m}{2(m + 2c)(m + c)} = 5
$$

---

### Step 5: Solve for Area of Triangle $ ABC $

Let’s consider a special configuration where this simplifies nicely. Assume:

- $ a = 0 $, so $ A = (0, b) $
- Then $ AB = b $, $ BC = 2c $
- $ m = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = b $

Then from the perpendicularity condition:

$$
b^2 = (0 + b)(c - 0) = bc \Rightarrow b = c
$$

So $ A = (0, c) $, $ B = (0, 0) $, $ C = (2c, 0) $

Now, the triangle $ ABC $ is a **right triangle** with legs of lengths $ c $ and $ 2c $, so:

$$
S_{ABC} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot c \cdot 2c = c^2
$$

Now compute $ S_{DEF} $ using the coordinates of $ D = (c, 0) $, $ E = \left( \frac{2c}{3}, \frac{2c}{3} \right) $, $ F = \left( \frac{c}{2}, \frac{c}{2} \right) $

Using the shoelace formula:

$$
S_{DEF} = \frac{1}{2} \left| c\left(\frac{2c}{3} - \frac{c}{2}\right) + \frac{2c}{3} \left(\frac{c}{2} - 0\right) + \frac{c}{2} \left(0 - \frac{2c}{3}\right) \right|
$$

$$
= \frac{1}{2} \left| \frac{c^2}{6} + \frac{c^2}{3} - \frac{c^2}{3} \right| = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{c^2}{6} = \frac{c^2}{12}
$$

Set this equal to 5:

$$
\frac{c^2}{12} = 5 \Rightarrow c^2 = 60
$$

Therefore, the area of triangle $ ABC $ is:

$$
S_{ABC} = c^2 = \boxed{60}
$$
