### Step 1: Use the identity for combining sine and cosine

Any expression of the form  
$$
A \sin x + B \cos x
$$  
can be rewritten as  
$$
R \sin(x + \phi),

$$
R = \sqrt{A^2 + B^2}
$$

\phi = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{B}{A}\right).

$$
R = \sqrt{1^2 + (\sqrt{3})^2} = \sqrt{1 + 3} = \sqrt{4} = 2.
$$

Therefore, the original function becomes:
$$
y = 2 \sin(x + \phi),
$$
for some phase angle $ \phi $, which we could compute explicitly if needed, but it's not necessary for finding the **maximum** value.

$$
