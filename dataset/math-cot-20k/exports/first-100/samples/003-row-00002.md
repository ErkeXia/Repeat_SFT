To solve the problem, we analyze the geometric construction step by step and determine the total area of all squares added at each stage.

---

### **Step 1: Initial Square**

We begin with a **4×4 square**.  
- **Area of step 1 square:**  
  $$
  A_1 = 4 \times 4 = 16
  $$

---

### **Step 2: Adding 1×1 Squares**

In step 2, we attach a **1×1 square** to each of the 4 sides of the original square.  
- **Number of new squares:** 4  
- **Area of each new square:** $1 \times 1 = 1$  
- **Total area added in step 2:**  
  $$
  A_2 = 4 \times 1 = 4
  $$

---

### **Step 3: Adding (1/4)×(1/4) Squares**

Each of the 4 squares from step 2 has **3 exposed sides**, and we attach a **(1/4)×(1/4)** square to the center of each.  
- **Number of new squares:** $4 \times 3 = 12$  
- **Area of each new square:** $\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{16}$  
- **Total area added in step 3:**  
  $$
  A_3 = 12 \times \frac{1}{16} = \frac{12}{16} = \frac{3}{4}
  $$

---

### **Step 4 and Beyond: General Pattern**

From step 4 onward, the process continues recursively:
- **Each new square added in step $n$** has 3 exposed sides.
- **Each square in step $n$** has side length that is $\frac{1}{4}$ of the previous step's.
- **Number of squares added in step $k$ (for $k \geq 2$):**  
  $$
  N_k = 4 \cdot 3^{k - 2}
  $$
- **Side length in step $k$:**  
  $$
  s_k = \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{k - 2}
  $$
- **Area per square in step $k$:**  
  $$
  a_k = \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{2(k - 2)} = \left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k - 2}
  $$
- **Total area added in step $k$:**  
  $$
  A_k = N_k \cdot a_k = 4 \cdot 3^{k - 2} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{16}\right)^{k - 2}
  $$

This gives us a geometric series starting from step 2:

$$
\sum_{k=2}^{\infty} A_k = \sum_{k=2}^{\infty} 4 \cdot \left(\frac{3}{16}\right)^{k - 2}
$$

Let’s simplify this by substituting $m = k - 2$, so the sum becomes:

$$
\sum_{m=0}^{\infty} 4 \cdot \left(\frac{3}{16}\right)^m = 4 \cdot \sum_{m=0}^{\infty} \left(\frac{3}{16}\right)^m
$$

This is a geometric series with first term 1 and common ratio $r = \frac{3}{16}$. The sum of the infinite geometric series is:

$$
\sum_{m=0}^{\infty} r^m = \frac{1}{1 - r} = \frac{1}{1 - \frac{3}{16}} = \frac{1}{\frac{13}{16}} = \frac{16}{13}
$$
