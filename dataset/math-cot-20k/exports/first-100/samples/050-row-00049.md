$$
[\sqrt[3]{1}] + [\sqrt[3]{2}] + \ldots + \left[\sqrt[3]{x^3 - 1}\right] = 400

we interpret $[ \cdot ]$ as the floor function, and aim to find a natural number $x$ such that the sum of the floor values of cube roots from 1 to $x^3 - 1$ equals 400.

### Step 1: Understanding the Structure of the Sum

For any integer $n$, the cube root of numbers from $n^3$ to $(n+1)^3 - 1$ will have a floor value of $n$. The number of such values is:

$$
(n+1)^3 - n^3 = 3n^2 + 3n + 1
$$

Thus, the sum of the floor values of cube roots from 1 to $x^3 - 1$ is:

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{x-1} n \cdot (3n^2 + 3n + 1)
$$

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{x-1} (3n^3 + 3n^2 + n)

- $\sum_{n=1}^{m} n = \frac{m(m+1)}{2}$
- $\sum_{n=1}^{m} n^2 = \frac{m(m+1)(2m+1)}{6}$
- $\sum_{n=1}^{m} n^3 = \left( \frac{m(m+1)}{2} \right)^2$
