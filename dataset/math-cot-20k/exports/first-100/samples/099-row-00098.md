To find the area of triangle $ \triangle ABC $, we are given the following:

- $ AB = 15 $
- $ AC = 13 $
- The altitude from vertex $ A $ to side $ BC $ is $ AD = 12 $
- $ \triangle ABC $ is an acute triangle

We are to find the area $ P $ of triangle $ ABC $.

---

### Step 1: Use the Altitude to Determine the Base $ BC $

Since $ AD $ is the altitude from vertex $ A $ to side $ BC $, it is perpendicular to $ BC $. Therefore, we can split triangle $ ABC $ into two right triangles: $ \triangle ABD $ and $ \triangle ACD $, each with a right angle at $ D $.

Let:
- $ BD = x $
- $ DC = y $

Then $ BC = x + y $, and using the Pythagorean Theorem in each right triangle:

1. In $ \triangle ABD $:  
   $$
   AB^2 = AD^2 + BD^2 \Rightarrow 15^2 = 12^2 + x^2 \Rightarrow x^2 = 225 - 144 = 81 \Rightarrow x = 9
   $$

2. In $ \triangle ACD $:  
   $$
   AC^2 = AD^2 + DC^2 \Rightarrow 13^2 = 12^2 + y^2 \Rightarrow y^2 = 169 - 144 = 25 \Rightarrow y = 5
   $$

So, $ BC = x + y = 9 + 5 = 14 $

---

### Step 2: Calculate the Area

Now that we know the base $ BC = 14 $ and the corresponding height $ AD = 12 $, we can use the standard area formula for a triangle:

$$
P = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \text{base} \cdot \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 14 \cdot 12 = \frac{168}{2} = 84
$$

---

### Step 3: Verification Using Heron’s Formula

To confirm, let’s apply **Heron’s Formula**:

- $ a = 15 $
- $ b = 13 $
- $ c = 14 $

Compute the semi-perimeter:
$$
s = \frac{15 + 13 + 14}{2} = 21

$$

This confirms our earlier result.

A triangle is acute if the square of each side is less than the sum of the squares of the other two sides.
